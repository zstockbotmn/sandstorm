STEP 3d1 (behind FX3 = /[?&]fx=3(?:&|$)/): distant skyline flashes and tracers in src/fx3.js ONLY (read it first: rg -n "ensurePool|poolCap|qName|api\." src/fx3.js). Do not touch src/audio.js in this piece.
1) api.skylineShot(near): near 0..1. Its own small pool, separate from the puffs: ONE THREE.InstancedMesh of 6 additive quads for flashes (MeshBasicMaterial, warm #ffd27a, AdditiveBlending, transparent, depthWrite false, fog false, the same soft round CanvasTexture as the puffs) and ONE InstancedMesh of 6 thin additive quads for tracers (same colour, no texture, opacity 0.8). Lazy-create like ensurePool.
2) Position: a point ahead of the player on the skyline: z = camera.position.z - (120 - 90 * near), x = random +-25, y = 3 + random 15 (rooftops). Flash: size 1.2 + 1.5 * near m, billboarded to the camera, life 0.08 s of sim time AND at least 2 rendered frames (window.SS.frameN, same pattern as the puffs), then scale 0. Tracer (60% of shots, never on Ultra-low): a quad 0.08 m wide, 6-12 m long, starting at the flash, oriented along a random mostly-horizontal direction (any heading, |y| <= 0.2), moving 300 m/s along it for 0.25 s or 2 rendered frames, whichever is later; face it to the camera around its long axis (simple: build the matrix from the direction and the camera vector).
3) Update both in the existing update(dt) with the same capped dt. Cheap: no allocations per shot (reuse vectors), no per-frame work when nothing is live.
4) window.SS.fx3.skyline = { flashes, tracers, lastZ } (counts since start, z of the last shot).
node --check src/fx3.js and node tools/build.js at the end.
