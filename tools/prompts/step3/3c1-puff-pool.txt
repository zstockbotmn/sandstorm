STEP 3c (behind FX3 = /[?&]fx=3(?:&|$)/): cheap instanced sprite puffs in src/fx3.js (exists from 3a; read it). Without ?fx=3 nothing changes.
This piece: the pool and its API only, no wiring into weapon/enemies yet.
1) fx.puffs: ONE THREE.InstancedMesh of camera-facing quads (PlaneGeometry 1x1, MeshBasicMaterial transparent, depthWrite false, vertexColors via instanceColor, a soft round alpha from a 32x32 CanvasTexture made in code). Pool size by tier: read window.SS.quality name or rg -n "qName|quality" src/quality.js: high 96, low 48, ultra-low 0 (pool not created). Each particle: pos, vel, life, size growth, colour, gravity; dead ones get scale 0. Billboard by copying the camera quaternion per instance. Update in fx.update(dt) with dt capped at 0.1 and life in sim time, plus a minimum of 2 rendered frames so at 1 fps a puff is still seen.
2) fx.dust(point, normal, n=6): tan sand-coloured (#c9b38a +-10%) puffs, size 0.15 -> 0.6 m over 0.6-0.9 s, velocity along normal 0.6-1.2 m/s + upward 0.4, gravity -0.6. fx.debris(point, normal, n=4): small dark chips (#5a4a3a, 0.04-0.07 m, 0.5 s, velocity 2-3 m/s, gravity -9.8). fx.impact(point, normal, surface) = dust + debris (plaster walls: lighter colour #d8cfc0).
3) window.SS.fx3.puffs = { live, pool, spawned } for tests.
node --check and node tools/build.js at the end.
