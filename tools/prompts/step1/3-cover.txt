Realistic COVER ENTRY/EXIT/PEEK, RELOADS and IDLE FIDGETS for enemy fighters (src/enemies.js phases enterCover/peek/hide/tickFire, proceduralLean, supportArm; src/locomotion.js blend layer). Upper-body layers are procedural (IK + bone bends after the mixer), legs come from the blend layer.
