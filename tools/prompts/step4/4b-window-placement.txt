STEP 4b (behind ?bld=4, after 4a): put the 4a interior windows on the Meshy art buildings (bldg1-4, src/world.js placeArtBuilding). The windows are baked into their textures, so per building keep a small hand table WINDOWS[name] = list of window rects in the model's own normalized street-face space (u along the facade 0..1, v up 0..1, w, h), written from the A5 High screenshots (logs/a5/tex_facade_high.png etc.) and checked with a raycast: for each rect, raycast from the street at its centre and place the quad 2 cm in front of the hit (skip it if the hit is more than 0.5 m behind the facade plane or misses). One InstancedMesh per segment (merge with the 4a list in seg._wins). Rects must sit inside the painted window openings, never over balconies or shutters (prefer fewer, correct windows). Low: same shader, but only windows on floors 1-2 (no top floor) and none beyond 45 m from the street centre line; Ultra-low: the flat fallback on floor 1 only. SS.bld4.windows counts them. node --check only.
